{% trans "About the East Sussex Historic Environment Record (ESHER)" %}

{% trans "ESHER is a record of archaeological sites and historical finds. It covers the areas of East Sussex and Brighton and Hove." %}

{% trans "It is a resource for researchers, local historians, archaeologists, and members of the public, and helps the County Council to manage the local historic environment, especially to make informed decisions on planning applications." %}

{% trans "It is part of a national network of Historic Environment Records (HERs), supported by Historic England." %}

{% trans "You can only use the data from ESHER for personal research. This information is not for commercial purposes or publication without prior permission." %}

{% trans "What information does the ESHER contain?" %}

{% trans "ESHER contains information on many archaeological periods. These range from Prehistoric finds through to sites from the 20th century." %}

{% trans "The Record includes large sites such as Lewes Castle and Battle Abbey, as well as lesser-known sites. Some of these sites are only identified by aerial photographs or Lidar analysis." %}

{% trans "There are over 21,000 archaeological and historical sites recorded on the ESHER. We also hold roughly 2,000 archaeological reports, aerial photographs and a library of archaeological and historical sources." %}

{% trans "The amount of information available on a site or find can vary. Some sites are only known from historical sources but modern excavations are more likely to have precise information." %}
